   response without a licence).

3. PWA installs, opens offline to a clear message, and updates cleanly.
4. Terms, Refund, and Privacy pages are live and linked.
5. Analytics, error tracking, and uptime alerting are active.
6. Tested on iOS Safari and a low-end Android browser — not only on
   desktop Chrome.
