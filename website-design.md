# OBI-ISM Companion Website Design Note

The companion website presents OBI-ISM as a **civic-editorial manifesto and field guide**, not a generic book landing page. Its visual vocabulary is shared with the reader: deep indigo institutional ground, acid-lime action signals, coral public-intervention geometry, electric-sky discovery accents, warm paper, oversized editorial serif typography, section numbering, rule lines, and the supplied book cover as the authentic visual anchor.

The redesigned website introduces a high-impact cover stage, interactive principle selector, practical entry cards into the manuscript, a reader preview, and a clear mobile-access pathway. Desktop review confirmed a strong manifesto hierarchy and a distinctive cover-led visual system. Mobile review at 390px confirmed that the compact header, primary calls to action, headline, color fields, and cover composition remain readable without overflow.

## Cinematic Nigerian-landmark landing screen

The OBI-ISM entry screen now opens on a calm eight-second, muted Nigerian-landmark video loop. The composition prioritizes the Lekki-Ikoyi Link Bridge and Lagos waterfront, with subtle Abuja civic architecture in the broader visual narrative. A deep indigo overlay preserves high contrast for the headline, calls to action, and book cover. The screen includes an explicit play/pause motion control, a location marker, and a generated cinematic poster fallback while the video initializes. Desktop and 390px mobile reviews confirmed readable copy, a clean overlay, and no visual overflow.

The landmark visibility was subsequently increased by reducing the overlay density and adding a dedicated, unmistakable landmark-video panel at the top of the native Home screen. The video now remains visible as a clear Lagos bridge backdrop on the website while copy continues to meet high-contrast readability requirements.
