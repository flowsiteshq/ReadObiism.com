# OBI-ISM Companion Website Design Note

The companion website presents OBI-ISM as a serious civic-philosophy publication, not a generic application landing page. Its visual vocabulary is shared with the reader: deep forest green, warm paper, antique gold, traditional serif display typography, vertical rules, editorial section numbering, and the supplied book cover as the authentic visual anchor.

The desktop review confirmed a composed book-launch hero, strong section hierarchy, a clear transition from book to philosophy to protected-reader access, and appropriate visual restraint. The mobile review confirmed the hero and primary calls to action remain legible at 390px. The mobile header was refined to show only the essential “Explore” and “Get app” routes, preventing nav overflow while retaining direct access to the primary reader journey.
