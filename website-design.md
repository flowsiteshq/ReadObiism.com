# OBI-ISM Companion Website Design Note

The companion website presents OBI-ISM as a **civic-editorial manifesto and field guide**, not a generic book landing page. Its visual vocabulary is shared with the reader: deep indigo institutional ground, acid-lime action signals, coral public-intervention geometry, electric-sky discovery accents, warm paper, oversized editorial serif typography, section numbering, rule lines, and the supplied book cover as the authentic visual anchor.

The redesigned website introduces a high-impact cover stage, interactive principle selector, practical entry cards into the manuscript, a reader preview, and a clear mobile-access pathway. Desktop review confirmed a strong manifesto hierarchy and a distinctive cover-led visual system. Mobile review at 390px confirmed that the compact header, primary calls to action, headline, color fields, and cover composition remain readable without overflow.
