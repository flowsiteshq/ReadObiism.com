Refund Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

OBI-ISM is a digital edition. Once access is activated, the edition can be read immediately. This policy explains how to request help with a purchase or a refund.

Requesting a refund

Contact us at sensei30002003@gmail.com from the email address used for purchase. Include your purchase email, purchase date, and a short description of the issue. We may request information needed to locate and verify the transaction.

How requests are assessed

We assess requests individually and in accordance with applicable consumer-protection law. We will consider, among other things, whether access could not be activated, whether there was an unauthorised or duplicate charge, and whether the edition materially failed to operate as described. Nothing in this policy removes rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Digital-access effect

If a refund is approved, access to the protected edition may be revoked and the associated device or entitlement may be deactivated. A refund is not a licence to retain, share, or redistribute the edition.

Timing and payment providers

We will acknowledge refund requests promptly and provide an outcome or a request for more information. Where a refund is approved, it is returned through the original payment route when possible. Processing time after approval depends on the payment provider and your financial institution.

Need account help instead?

If you cannot sign in or have moved to a new device, contact support before submitting a refund request; an account or device issue may be resolved without cancelling your edition.
