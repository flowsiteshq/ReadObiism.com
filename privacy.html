OBI-ISM · PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

What this policy covers

This policy explains how OBI-ISM handles information when you use the OBI-ISM digital reading application and its browser edition, readobiism.com.

Information we handle

Depending on the features you use, OBI-ISM may handle your name and email address for authentication and support, purchase and entitlement records, device and session information used to protect one-device access, reading-position and preference data, and technical information needed to keep the service secure and reliable.

How information is used

We use this information to authenticate readers, verify purchases, provide access to the protected edition, enforce entitlement and device controls, save reading preferences, respond to support requests, prevent fraud and unauthorized sharing, and maintain the service.

Payments

Android purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. Browser-edition purchases are processed through Stripe. OBI-ISM does not place payment-card numbers in the mobile app or browser reader.

Sharing and security

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that host authentication, databases, payments, storage, or application delivery. Data is transmitted using HTTPS where supported, and access decisions are enforced on the server.

Retention and deletion

We retain account, transaction, and entitlement records only as reasonably necessary to provide access, prevent fraud, meet legal or accounting obligations, and resolve disputes. Request deletion at readobiism.com/delete-account.html.

Children

OBI-ISM is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
