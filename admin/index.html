Forensic watermark

Decode an invisible copy identifier from leaked text.

INTERNAL

Every protected chapter carries an invisible zero-width identifier. Paste copied reader text below to identify the purchase.

HOW IT WORKS

Invisible, not absent.

The identifier is made of zero-width characters, so the passage looks ordinary. It survives copy and paste, but retyping or some formatting tools can strip it.

Paste a passage and decode it to see the matched purchase.

Copy is disabled inside the reader to prevent casual sharing. Use Load a sample to test the decoder.
